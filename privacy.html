Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026
Strict No-Logging Commitment: We do not log, store, sell, or share your browsing activity, DNS queries, visited websites, used apps, or traffic contents. We never track or transmit your actual IP address or connection timestamps to any of our servers.

1. Overview and Core Functionality

Our VPN application uses Android's official VpnService API to establish an encrypted OpenVPN tunnel between your device and a secure VPN server of your choice. While connected, your device's internet traffic travels through that encrypted tunnel, masking your public IP address from the websites and online services you access.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

Your privacy and digital autonomy are paramount. We strictly enforce a no-logs architecture:

  • We do not monitor, record, log, or store your browsing history or traffic payloads.
  • We do not intercept, alter, or redirect your network requests or DNS queries.
  • We do not collect or transmit your real IP address to our servers.
  • We do not record connection timestamps or session durations on our servers.
  • We do not employ any third-party behavioral analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that track user identities.

3. On-Device Data Storage

Certain functional data remains exclusively stored on your local device and is never transmitted to external servers:

  • Session Counters: Real-time connection status and byte counters are processed entirely on-device to display session statistics in the UI.
  • Split Tunneling: If you use split tunneling, the list of installed applications is read locally solely to allow you to select which apps bypass or use the VPN tunnel. This list never leaves your device.
  • Preferences: Your selected server preferences, language selection, and consent state are stored locally via Android shared preferences.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Free access to VPN servers is supported by advertisements served through Google AdMob:

  • Ads are served directly by Google AdMob according to Google Play Developer Policies.
  • Advertisements are never injected into, modified by, or routed through the VPN tunnel.
  • User consent for advertising in regulated regions (such as the EEA and UK) is obtained through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

5. Security

All VPN tunnels utilize industry-standard OpenVPN encryption protocols with TLS to safeguard your data in transit, particularly on unsecure public Wi-Fi networks.
